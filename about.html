About Trailhead Standard

A Texas company building practical financial tools for real people.

Who We Are

Trailhead Standard, LLC is a Texas Limited Liability Company focused on building practical, affordable financial tools for everyday people — working professionals, families, and individuals who want financial clarity without the complexity or cost of enterprise software.

We believe the best financial tools are ones that are simple to use, honest about what they do, and affordable enough for everyone. That philosophy drives every product we build.

Our Flagship Brand: MyClearBudget

MyClearBudget is our primary product line — a growing suite of spreadsheet-based financial tools available as one-time downloads. No subscriptions. No bank connections required. No monthly fees. You buy it, you own it.

Products in the MyClearBudget suite include personal budget trackers, debt payoff calculators, savings goal trackers, freelance income tools, and a full Real Estate Investor Toolkit for property investors.

All products are available at myclearbudget.com.
