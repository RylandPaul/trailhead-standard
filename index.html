About Trailhead Standard

Built by real people,
for real budgets.

Trailhead Standard, LLC is a Texas-based company focused on building practical financial tools for working people — people who want to understand their money without paying monthly fees or connecting bank accounts.

Our flagship brand, MyClearBudget, offers a growing suite of spreadsheet tools that help individuals and families take control of their finances, pay off debt, and plan for the future.

We believe financial clarity should be simple, honest, and affordable.
