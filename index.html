Trailhead Standard — Maintenance Intake & Dispatch

Maintenance requests, handled around the clock.

Trailhead Standard, LLC provides maintenance intake and dispatch services for residential property management companies. Tenants reach us by text message or email at any hour, and every request is documented, triaged, and dispatched to the property manager's own approved vendors.

💬

Text & Email Intake

Tenants text our maintenance number or send an email to submit a request. Every request becomes a tracked work order, with a confirmation sent back to the tenant.

🔍

Triage & Follow-Up

Each request is assessed for urgency, emergencies first. If details are missing, we ask the tenant follow-up questions before dispatch, so vendors arrive prepared.

🔧

Dispatch to Your Vendors

Work is dispatched to the property management company's own approved vendors under its rules, with scheduling updates to the tenant until the job is complete.

🏢

For Property Managers

Built for residential property management companies managing 200 to 500 doors. To see the system, contact us at trailheadintakedispatch@gmail.com.
