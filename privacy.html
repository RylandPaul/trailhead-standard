Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Trailhead Standard, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit trailheadstandard.com or use our products under the MyClearBudget brand.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate our business:

  • Contact information — name and email address when you contact us or subscribe to our mailing list.
  • Purchase information — processed by third-party platforms (Etsy, Payhip, Stripe). We do not store payment card data.
  • Website analytics — anonymized usage data via Google Analytics (page views, traffic sources). No personally identifiable information is collected in analytics.

How We Use Your Information

  • To fulfill product purchases and deliver digital downloads
  • To respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • To send product updates and marketing emails (only with your consent)
  • To improve our products and website

Our Products — Your Data Stays Local

All MyClearBudget spreadsheet products are downloaded files that operate entirely on your local device. We do not collect, transmit, or access any financial data you enter into our spreadsheet tools. Your financial information never leaves your computer.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Etsy — marketplace sales platform
  • Payhip — digital product delivery
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • MailerLite — email marketing
  • Google Analytics — website analytics
  • Formspree — contact form processing

Cookies

Our website uses cookies for analytics purposes (Google Analytics). You may disable cookies in your browser settings. We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking across other websites.

Data Retention

We retain contact and email information as long as necessary to maintain our business relationship with you. You may request deletion at any time by emailing us.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time
