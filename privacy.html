Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Trailhead Standard, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit trailheadstandard.com or use our services.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate our business:

  • Contact information — name and email address when you contact us or subscribe to our mailing list.
  • Purchase information — processed by third-party platforms (Gumroad, Stripe). We do not store payment card data.
  • Client documents — project documents you send us for a Project Health Check review, used only to perform that review.
  • Website analytics — anonymized usage data via Google Analytics (page views, traffic sources). No personally identifiable information is collected in analytics.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the services and digital products you requested
  • To respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • To send updates about your service or purchase (we run no marketing lists)
  • To improve our products and website

Client Documents — Confidential

Project documents sent to us for a Project Health Check are treated as confidential. They are used only to perform your review, are never shared outside the engagement, and are never used for marketing.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Gumroad — digital product delivery
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Google Analytics — website analytics
  • Formspree — contact form processing
  • Twilio — text messaging delivery for our maintenance intake program

Cookies

Our website uses cookies for analytics purposes (Google Analytics). You may disable cookies in your browser settings. We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking across other websites.

Data Retention

We retain contact and email information as long as necessary to maintain our business relationship with you. You may request deletion at any time by emailing us.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time

Text Messaging (SMS) Program

Trailhead Standard, LLC also provides maintenance intake and dispatch services for residential property management companies. If you are a tenant of a participating property management company, you may communicate with us by text message about maintenance requests for your home.

Through the messaging program we collect your name, mobile phone number, rental property address and unit number, the contents of your maintenance requests (including the messages and photos you send us), and records of messages sent and received, including opt-in and opt-out records.

We use this information to receive, document, and route your maintenance request, to communicate with you about it (including confirmations, follow-up questions, scheduling updates, and completion notices), and to operate and improve the service. We share it only with the property management company that manages your home and the vendors assigned to your request, and when required by law.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Message frequency varies depending on your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, and HELP for help. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of your lease or of receiving maintenance service. For questions about messaging privacy, contact us at trailheadintakedispatch@gmail.com.
