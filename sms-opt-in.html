Maintenance Text Messages: How Tenants Opt In

Trailhead Standard LLC provides maintenance intake and dispatch for residential property management companies. Tenants of participating property managers opt in to text messages about their maintenance requests through the card below. The card is printed in tenant lease documents and move-in packets provided by the property management company. This page reproduces that card exactly as tenants see it in print.

Need maintenance? Text (737) 271-0967. By texting, you agree to receive texts about your request from Trailhead Standard LLC. Msg frequency varies. Msg and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help.

The first reply a tenant receives

"Trailhead Standard Maintenance: You are signed up for texts about your maintenance requests. Msg frequency varies. Msg and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel."

Messaging is two-way and about that tenant's maintenance request only. Message frequency varies, typically 2 to 10 messages per request. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.
